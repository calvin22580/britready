Privacy Policy

Life in UK Practice · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how the Life in UK Practice iOS app ("the app"), called "Practice: Life in UK Test" (followed by the year) on the App Store, handles your information. In short, the app collects no personal data unless you choose to give it: it sends anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off, and your email address only if you ask for a question a day by email.

Who we are

The app is published by Calvin Holloway, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. Postal address: 128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom. Contact: support@steadylamp.com.

Information we collect

The app has no user accounts and doesn't send your answers, progress or settings to us or to anyone else. It shows no ads and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Anonymous usage counts

From version 1.2 the app sends anonymous counts of how it is used, so we can see which features help and fix what doesn't. These are events such as "app opened", "practice started" or "mock test finished" (with the kind of practice, the number of questions and the score), "purchase screen shown", "plan bought" (weekly, monthly or lifetime), and changes to the help language or appearance. Each comes with the app and iOS versions, the language you chose for the notes and your phone's main language (just the language, such as "English", not your region), and a random id made by the app on your device, which isn't linked to you, your Apple Account or your device's own identifiers, and is removed if you delete the app.

They don't include your name, email, location, answers to individual questions, test date or anything you type. We don't use them for advertising, don't build profiles of people and don't sell or share them.

They are processed for us by PostHog Inc. (posthog.com), on servers in the United States, under the UK's data protection safeguards for international transfers. PostHog is set not to store IP addresses or work out a location. We keep the counts for up to 12 months. Our basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

To turn them off, go to Settings → Privacy in the app and turn off "Share anonymous usage data". Nothing more is sent from then on.

From version 1.5, when you buy Full Access while "Share anonymous usage data" is on, the app gives Apple the same random id with the purchase, so Apple's notices to us about renewals, cancelled free trials and refunds can be matched to the anonymous counts. It isn't sent with purchases while that setting is off.

Feedback you send

From version 1.5, if you write something in the app's feedback box and tap Send, the text is sent to us through PostHog (as above), with the same anonymous details as the usage counts, such as the app version, so we can improve the app. If "Share anonymous usage data" is off, it goes with a one-off random id instead of this install's, so it can't be joined to anything else. It isn't linked to your name or email, so we can't reply; please don't include personal details. It is sent only when you tap Send, whether or not "Share anonymous usage data" is on. We keep feedback for up to 12 months. Our basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Question of the day by email

From version 1.5, Settings has a switch, "Email me a question a day". It is off unless you turn it on, and the app never asks for your email address anywhere else. If you turn it on and enter your email address, the app sends the address to our server and we email you a link to confirm it is yours. Once you confirm, we send one practice question a day to that address. We use the address only for this. We don't use it for advertising by anyone else, and we don't sell or share it. It isn't joined to the anonymous usage counts or to your progress, which stays on your device.

Our basis is your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time: use the unsubscribe link in any of the emails, turn the switch off in Settings, or email us. We keep your address while you're subscribed. If you unsubscribe, or never confirm, we delete it within 30 days. The address is stored by Supabase (in the European Union) and the emails are sent by Resend (from servers in the European Union); both act only on our behalf. The emails come from hello@lifeintheukquestions.co.uk, the address of our website, Life in the UK Questions, which runs the same service.

Measuring our Google ads

From version 1.5, if "Share anonymous usage data" is on, the app also tells Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) when it is first opened and when Full Access is bought, with the plan and price. Google's software also records basic app events by itself, such as when the app is opened (sessions), how long it is used, and app and system updates, with the device model and app and system versions. This lets Google tell us how many people who found the app through our Google ads went on to install it and buy, so we can stop paying for ads that don't work. Google receives a random id made by its software for this install of the app and your IP address, from which it works out an approximate location (city level). It doesn't receive your name, email, answers, test date or your device's advertising identifier. The app doesn't ask to track you and isn't linked to other apps' data. Google processes this under its terms for Google Analytics for Firebase and may store it outside the UK under the UK's data protection safeguards for international transfers. The data isn't used to personalise ads. Google keeps it for 2 months for exploration reports, and we keep the totals. Our basis is our legitimate interest in knowing which advertising works (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Turning off "Share anonymous usage data" stops this too.

If you choose to email us, for example with "Report a problem" under a question, your email app sends us your message, your email address and the details it shows (such as the question's ID and the app and iOS versions). We use them only to reply and to fix the question, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you and correcting the app (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), and we don't share them with anyone else. Our email is hosted by Apple (iCloud Mail), which handles it only on our behalf and may store it outside the UK under the UK's data protection safeguards for international transfers.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • your answers, study progress, saved questions and mock test results;
  • your settings, such as your test date, reminder time and haptics preference;
  • whether you have Full Access.

This data stays on your device, apart from the anonymous counts above and being included in your own iCloud or computer backups if you use them. Settings → Reset all progress in the app clears your answers, progress, saved questions and test results (your test date and settings stay). Deleting the app removes all of it.

Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions, including renewals, cancellations and refunds, are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Neither the app nor we receive your payment details, and we don't receive your name or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules them locally on your device. No push notification service is used and no data is sent anywhere.

Children

The app is a general study aid and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

If you email us, we keep the conversation for up to 12 months after it ends and then delete it. You can ask us to see, correct or delete what we hold, or object to or restrict how we use it, by emailing us. Everything else stays on your device, under your control. If you have any questions, contact us at the address above. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above. If we ever start collecting more, we'll update the policy and the app's App Store privacy details before doing so.
