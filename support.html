Frequently asked questions

Is this the official test?

No. Life in UK Practice is an independent study aid. Our questions are original and are based on the handbook for the test, Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents (3rd edition). For official information and to book the test, visit gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test.

What's free and what's included in Full Access?

Free: two full mock tests, a selection of practice questions from every topic, and the study notes for Chapters 1 and 2. Full Access unlocks every question, every mock test, unlimited random tests, all study notes and flashcards, the key dates timeline, and read-aloud for everything.

What are the Full Access plans?

There are three, and each one unlocks exactly the same Full Access:

  • Weekly: £2.99 a week. A subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it.
  • Monthly: £8.99 a month. A subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it.
  • Lifetime: £14.99, paid once. It never renews and is yours for good, and you can share it with your family through Family Sharing.

Prices are for the UK and may vary in other countries. Payment is taken from your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current week or month. There are no free trials.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Life in UK Practice and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also use Apple's manage subscriptions page, or Settings → Manage subscription in the app.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current week or month to avoid paying for the next one. You keep Full Access until the end of the period you've paid for, and your progress stays on your device.

Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. If you switch to Lifetime, cancel your subscription too, so it doesn't renew.

I bought Full Access but it's locked on my new device

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the app while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy it. This works for all three plans. If you bought Lifetime and use Family Sharing, family members can restore it too. Subscriptions aren't shared with family members.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. To stop a subscription renewing, cancel it as described above.

Is my progress backed up?

Progress is stored on your device and included in your normal iCloud or computer backups. It isn't synced between devices.

I think an answer is wrong

Please tell us: tap "Report a problem" under the question's explanation, which starts an email with the question's details. We'll check it against the handbook. Note that the test follows the handbook, which was written in 2013. Where real life has changed since, the app shows a note explaining the difference.
